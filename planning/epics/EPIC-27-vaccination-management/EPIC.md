# EPIC-27: Vaccination Management

## Overview
**Goal**: Track vaccinations per animal: schedule, administer, record, and generate vaccination certificates.
**Why it matters**: Vaccination records are legally required for adoption and are a key part of animal welfare.
**Target users**: Veterinarians, shelter staff, adopters

- [ ] [S4] Vaccination certificate PDF generation (5 pts, P1, Fullstack)
