# EPIC-92: Education & Responsible Pet Ownership Hub

## Overview

This epic creates a comprehensive educational hub with articles, videos, and resources about pet care, training, health, and responsible ownership. Features include article management with categories, public content hub, video support, required pre-adoption reading, sterilization awareness campaign, and admin content creation tools.

## Why This Epic Matters

Education is fundamental to reducing animal suffering. By providing accessible, high-quality information about pet care, training, and responsible ownership, the organization empowers adopters to provide better care, reduce abandonment, and promote sterilization awareness. The platform becomes a trusted resource for the entire Paraguayan animal lover community.

## Target Users

Prospective adopters learning about responsible pet ownership, current pet owners seeking care advice, volunteers understanding animal behavior, and the general public seeking educational content.

## Scope: In Scope

Article model with categories and content. Public learning hub. Video embed support. Required pre-adoption reading. Sterilization awareness campaign page. Admin content editor with rich text and media support. Article scheduling and publication. SEO optimization.

## Scope: Out of Scope

Live video streaming. Interactive courses or certifications. Veterinary consultation chatbot. Translation to multiple languages (Spanish only for now).

## Stories

7 stories: S1 implements article model, S2 creates public learning hub, S3 adds article detail pages, S4 implements required pre-adoption reading, S5 creates sterilization campaign, S6 adds video support, S7 creates admin editor.

## Dependencies

Requires database, user authentication, rich text editor library, and video embed capability.

## Success Metrics

Hub succeeds when 80% of adopters read required articles, sterilization page drives 30% increase in castration campaign donations, and hub attracts 10K monthly visitors.
