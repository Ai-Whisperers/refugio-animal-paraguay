# S5: Animal detail view with full history

## Story
As a **staff member**, I want **animal detail view with full history** so that **shelter operations are efficient and data-driven**.

## Description
Comprehensive animal detail page showing all information and history timeline.

## Acceptance Criteria

**Given** Given I click on an animal in the list
**When** When the detail page loads
**Then** Then I see all animal data, photos, status history, medical summary, and related adoption requests

**Given** Given I view the history tab
**When** When I scroll
**Then** Then I see a chronological timeline of all events for this animal
